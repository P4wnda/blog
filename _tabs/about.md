I am p4wnda, just a dude who likes hacking stuff, breaking labs, and figuring out how things actually work.

This blog is where I collect writeups, notes, and techniques from Hack The Box, OverTheWire, certificates, private labs, and whatever else I end up digging into. I try to keep things direct: what worked, what failed, and what I learned from it.

Most posts are written for my future self first, but if they help someone else understand an attack path or avoid getting stuck, even better.
